# AWS

Most popular and widely used cloud platform
IT is Infrastructure as a service platform 
allows us to configure infra in granular level. 

Used to host complex apps. 

## Intro

Collection of the services, grouped into categories. 

Most common: 
EC2, Storage: S3, Databases, VPS, Identity, Containers

## Scopes of Services

There are three scopes:  
1. Global scope, such as IAM, billing, Route53
2. Regional: S3, VPC, DynamoDB...
3. Availability zone: EC2, EBS, RDS
